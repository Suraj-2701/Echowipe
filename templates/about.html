Our Mission to Secure Truth

In an era of deepfakes and AI manipulation, Echowipe provides the tools necessary to verify audio authenticity and restore trust in digital communication.

Who We Are

Echowipe was founded by a team of cybersecurity experts and AI researchers dedicated to fighting voice cloning fraud. We realized that as AI voices became indistinguishable from humans, the world needed a "digital fingerprint" scanner for sound.

Today, our platform processes thousands of samples daily, helping journalists, security firms, and individuals verify the voices they hear.
