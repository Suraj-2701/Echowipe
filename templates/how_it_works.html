Behind the Scenes of Echowipe

Learn how we utilize Signal Processing and Artificial Intelligence to verify audio authenticity.

1

Data Ingestion

Audio is uploaded and normalized to a standard sample rate (22.05kHz) to ensure consistency across different microphone qualities.

2

Feature Extraction

We extract MFCCs (Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients) which represent the short-term power spectrum of a sound.

3

Neural Inference

Our CNN (Convolutional Neural Network) model processes the spectral data, looking for "Phase Incoherence" found in AI voices.

4

Probability Scoring

The model outputs a Softmax probability score between 0 and 1, determining the final Real vs Fake classification.

Deepfake vs. Human Speech

AI models often fail to replicate the complex biological noise found in human lungs and vocal cords.

Human Voice

Contains natural jitter, varied harmonic richness, and organic breathing pauses that AI struggle to mimic.

Synthetic (AI) Voice

Often shows "robotic regularity" in the spectral peaks and lacks the micro-tremors of a biological vocal tract.

Advanced Forensic Technology

Our engine is built on a custom architecture optimized for low-latency detection without sacrificing accuracy.

  • Mel-Spectrogram Analysis: Converts time-domain audio into frequency-domain images for AI vision processing.
  • Artifact Detection: Identifies "griffin-lim" artifacts common in older TTS (Text-to-Speech) engines.
  • Universal Compatibility: Effective against ElevenLabs, RVC, and OpenAI voice models.
