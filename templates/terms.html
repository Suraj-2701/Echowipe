EchoWipe Terms & Conditions

Welcome to EchoWipe, a Synthetic Voice Detection System designed to identify AI-generated audio and protect users from voice deepfake attacks.

1. Purpose

This system is developed for cybersecurity awareness, research, and educational purposes to help detect AI-generated voice fraud.

2. User Responsibility

Users must upload only audio files they have permission to analyze. The system must not be used for illegal, harmful, or malicious activities.

3. Privacy

Uploaded audio files are processed only for detection analysis. The system does not permanently store user audio without permission.

4. Accuracy

The detection results are based on machine learning predictions and may not always be 100% accurate.

5. Prohibited Use

Users must not misuse the system, attempt to exploit it, or violate privacy and intellectual property rights.

6. Updates

These terms may be updated in the future as the system improves.
